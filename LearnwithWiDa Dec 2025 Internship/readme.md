## 🏆 Week 5 Capstone: Electronics Store Sales Analysis
The culmination of the internship was a practical business case analyzing 100 synthetic transaction records for an electronics retailer to uncover regional performance and customer behavior. 

### 🤝 Collaboration Note
This capstone was executed as part of **"Team Wicart."**  While we collaborated on the overarching business strategy and presentation narrative, the present workbook was my personal take on the project while the final reprt was a group effort.

### 🛠️ Technical Skills Demonstrated
* **Data Aggregation:** Extensive use of Pivot Tables to summarize complex metrics across regions, categories, and member tiers.
* **Dashboard Design:** Built a simple yet dynamic, user-friendly Excel Dashboard integrating KPIs, bar charts, line charts, and pie charts.
* **Business Storytelling:** Translated raw data into actionable recommendations for stakeholders.

### 📊 Capstone Key Insights
* **Regional Dominance:** The East region led all markets, generating $23,245 in sales, closely followed by the West ($22,746).
* **Product Performance:** Laptops were the primary revenue driver, contributing ~$40k to the total $87,876 sales volume.
* **Customer Loyalty:** "Gold" tier members generated the most revenue ($34,294), indicating strong brand loyalty among premium users.
* **Logistics:** 53% of customers opted for Standard shipping, prioritizing cost-efficiency over speed (Same Day: 19%).

### 💡 Strategic Recommendations
Based on the analysis, we recommended focusing marketing efforts on underperforming regions (North and South) and creating targeted incentives to encourage "Bronze" members to upgrade to "Gold" tiers to increase overall order values.
